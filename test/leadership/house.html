Leadership

House Leadership is elected every two years at the beginning of a new Congress. The Speaker of the House is elected by the entire membership of the House of Representatives, and other majority party and minority party leaders are selected by Members of each party's delegation. The Democratic Caucus is the formal organization of Democratic Members; the Republican Conference is the formal organization of Republican Members in the House.
