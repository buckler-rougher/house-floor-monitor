Accessibility

Effective November 6, 2026

Standard

This site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2, level AA. No third party has audited this site, and we make no claim that it conforms.

Keyboard

This site can be used with a keyboard alone. Dialogs, including bill details, vote recommendations, the time machine, and the suggestion box, move focus inside when they open, keep Tab and Shift+Tab inside while they are open, and return focus to where you were when they close. Escape closes them.

Screen readers

This site gives its buttons, links, and status marks text labels. Panel symbols are decorative and are not read aloud. Changes to a vote's tallies are announced politely, without interrupting what you are reading.

Motion

This site follows your device's reduced motion setting for expanding lists, the time machine, and the contents list.

Video and captions

This site shows floor video supplied by the chambers. Captions are the chambers' own and appear when the chamber provides them. We do not write or correct them.

Known limitations

This site has gaps. The chamber map is a drawing, and the time machine's travel animation is purely visual. Most live updates other than vote tallies are not announced to screen readers. The page is dense, and its type is small at the default size.

Feedback

If something on this site is hard or impossible to use, tell us. Say what you were trying to do and what got in the way. We will look into it and write back if you leave an email address.

Changes

This statement may be updated from time to time. The effective date above reflects the most recent revision.
