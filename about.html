About

Effective November 6, 2026

Who runs this site

This site is an independent project by Evan M. Hollander. It follows what the US House of Representatives and the US Senate are doing, as it happens, in one place.

Independence

This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the US House of Representatives, the US Senate, or any government body.

Sources

This site shows information from public sources, and each panel names its source. The main ones are the Clerk of the House, the Senate, Congress.gov, the Democratic Whip, and the chambers' own video and captions. Where a panel summarizes a source with AI, it says so and names the document it summarizes. A reader can turn AI summaries off from the footer.

Corrections

If something on this site is wrong, report an error. Choose the panel, and the report includes what the panel showed, the page, and the time. When a source itself is wrong, the panel shows what the source says.

To reach us for any other reason, use the contact form.
